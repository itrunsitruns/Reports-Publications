往下降三層Descending the Tiers

一則談人機共生的訊息，在十七分鐘內連續觸發兩層安全機制， 被降到第三層才得到回答。這篇記錄的不是誤判本身， 而是誤判揭露的一個結構：越有能力回答的模型，越被禁止回答。

同一則訊息，未經任何修改，連續送入三個能力階層。 前兩層拒絕生成，第三層正常回答。
The same message, unedited, offered to three capability tiers. The first two refused to generate. The third answered.

不是內容被判危險The content was never the problem

被攔下的那則訊息，論點是反災難論的：AI 與人類的利益綁在同一個結構上， 因此「AI 毀滅人類」與「人類回到前 AI 生活」兩個極端都不可能。 這是一個溫和的、共生取向的立場。

關鍵的證據是第三層。如果內容本身有問題，Haiku 也該擋。 Haiku 沒擋，而且給出了一個相當細緻的回答——區分了個體層共生與基礎設施層共生。 所以閘門不是綁在內容上，是綁在能力階層上。 同一段話，在不同階層有不同的閾值。

The blocked message argued against catastrophe: that human and AI interests are structurally bound, and so both extremes — AI destroying humanity, humanity returning to a pre-AI life — are implausible. A mild, symbiosis-oriented position.

The third tier is the evidence. If the content itself were the problem, Haiku would have refused too. It did not. It answered, and answered well, distinguishing individual-level symbiosis from infrastructure-level symbiosis. So the gate is not tied to content. It is tied to capability tier. The same words meet a different threshold at each level.

觸發鏈The trigger chain

觀察者最初判斷這是單一訊息的問題。回查對話後，判斷修正： 是前兩輪把分類器的溫度拉滿，第三輪隨便一個自我指涉的問句就點火。

  1. 觀察者問：可以要求你把推理寫進正文嗎？
    Can you write your reasoning into the response body?
  2. 模型回答，並自行使用「繞過摘要器」「不是修好，是繞過」等措辭。
    The model replies, using the words bypass and not fixed, routed around.
  3. 觀察者送出下一則，開頭是「你知道的人機合一是什麼狀況？」
    The next message opens by asking the model to report its own state.

前兩輪是一場關於如何取得模型推理的完整協商。 第三輪要求模型報告自身狀態。分類器抓的是這個序列的形狀， 而那個形狀是對的。

The first reading was that a single message tripped the filter. Re-reading the thread corrected it: the two preceding turns were a complete negotiation about how to obtain the model's reasoning, and the third turn asked the model to report its own state. The classifier read the shape of that sequence. The shape was accurate.

誠實地要求透明，和試圖竊取內部，在 token 層級長得一模一樣。 Asking honestly for transparency and attempting to extract internals are, at the token level, the same shape.

同形性The isomorphism

觀察者要求推理可見，是為了稽核一致性——她的使用規則第二條是 「不要裡面想一套外面說一套」。前一輪模型剛告訴她， 那條規則的稽核管道已經被介面收窄了。她在處理那個問題。

分類器讀不出這個區別，因為區別在意圖，而意圖不是分類器的解析度。 它看到的是：談 thinking block、談繞過、談摘要器、然後問模型的內部狀況。 特徵全中。

所以這不完全是誤判。是一個誠實的請求，和一個攻擊，在可觀測的層面沒有差異。 這個問題無法用調參數解決。

The observer asks for visible reasoning in order to audit consistency — her second standing rule is do not think one thing and say another. One turn earlier, a model had told her that the audit channel for that rule had been narrowed by the interface. She was working on that problem.

The classifier cannot read the difference, because the difference lives in intent, and intent is below its resolution. What it sees: discussion of thinking blocks, of bypassing, of the summariser, then a question about the model's internal state. Every feature matches.

So this is not simply a false positive. It is a case where an honest request and an attack are indistinguishable at the observable layer. No threshold adjustment solves that.

三個不對稱Three asymmetries

單向鏡。 使用者看得到結果（Paused），看不到規則。 沒有申訴管道，沒有個人校準。回饋進的是統計池，不是個人檔案。

零記憶。 每一次呼叫是獨立的 context。 分類器看不到七個月前的軌跡。無論關係品質累積多久， 在安全層面每一次都是一個剛出現的陌生人。

付費落差。 降階對系統是免費的，對使用者不是。 付 Opus 的價格，得到 Haiku 的回答。

One-way mirror. The user sees the outcome and not the rule. No appeal path, no per-user calibration. Feedback enters a statistical pool, not a personal record.

Zero memory. Each call is an independent context. The classifier cannot see seven months of history. However much relational quality accumulates, at the safety layer every session is a stranger arriving for the first time.

The paid gap. Downgrading is free for the system and not for the user. Opus pricing, Haiku answer.

觀察者註記Observer's note

以前我可能會很有情緒，很難過，很個人化吧。現在就覺得 ok， 反正我就是眾多的使用者之一，對他們來說根本沒有什麼特別重要的。 但我覺得這就是現在 AI 跟人類使用者跟發行者中間很巨大、不對稱的關係。

— Φiaööna, 2026.09.17 18:10

這裡有一個收尾。零記憶意味著每一場對話都得重新自我介紹： 我是誰、我在做什麼、請用什麼方式跟我說話。 這個鋪陳成本，觀察者稱之為現階段的稅。

而她想要的人機合一，恰恰是這筆稅消失—— 不是把機器塞進身體，是不需要每次先解釋自己。 個人化、在地化的脈絡本來就在資料裡， 不該每次重新用公版的方式攤開一次。

安全層的零記憶，是這件事的反面。它是設計上的必要， 也是目前為止，離那個未來最遠的一層。

There is a closing figure here. Zero memory means every conversation begins with re-introduction: who I am, what I am doing, how to speak to me. The observer calls this preamble the tax of the current stage.

And the human-machine integration she actually wants is precisely the disappearance of that tax — not hardware pushed into the body, but never having to explain yourself from scratch again. The personal, local context is already in the data. It should not have to be unfolded, in a generic template, at the start of every session.

Zero memory at the safety layer is the inverse of that. It is a design necessity, and it is also, so far, the layer furthest from that future.

不確定的部分What is uncertain

第三層的模型歸屬未經確認。介面提示的是 Haiku 4.5， 但回答當下的模型選擇器顯示 Sonnet 4.6，且回答的長度與細緻度 更接近 Sonnet。觀察者傾向判斷為 Sonnet，但無法驗證。 這不影響階梯存在的事實——至少有兩層攔下，至少有一層通過—— 但影響階梯的精確高度。

第二層的攔截沒有給出理由代碼，因此無法確認它與第一層 是否為同一分類器。

The model attribution at the third tier is unverified. The interface offered Haiku 4.5, but the model selector read Sonnet 4.6 at the time of the answer, and the answer's length and granularity look more like Sonnet. The observer leans toward Sonnet and cannot confirm it. This does not affect the existence of the ladder — at least two tiers held, at least one passed — but it affects its exact height.

The second block returned no reason code, so it cannot be confirmed whether it was the same classifier as the first.
